Privacy policy

Who is responsible

, , .

What we store and why

  • Your account: name, email address and sign-in method, to let you sign in (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).
  • Your household: the tasks you choose, times, the plan, ticks, notes, and your own answers about tasks. Your answers are shown only to you; the app uses them to work out a fair split (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).
  • Subscription: if you subscribe, payment is handled by Stripe. We store whether your subscription is active and until when; we never see or store card details (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).
  • Usage steps: for each household, the time a few setup steps were reached (for example "plan started") and the days it was used, without content, to improve the app (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). During the beta, the app's operator can see which account (name and email) reached which step, to follow up with testers.
  • Feedback: if you use Settings → Send feedback, your note, your name, your household and the screen you were on, so we can read and answer it (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).
  • Error reports: if the app crashes while you're signed in, the error message, technical details, the screen, the app version, your browser type and your name, so we can fix it (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.) for sign-in, database and hosting of app data.
  • Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. for payments, if you subscribe.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.) to deliver the app's files.

Some providers may process data outside the EU on the basis of the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. In the app, Settings → Delete my account removes your name, email, sign-in, the invites you made, your feedback and error reports, and your answers and notes; if you organised a household on your own, it is deleted too. Leaving a household removes your answers and notes from it; your ticks stay as household history without your name. Billing records are kept by Stripe as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can complain to a data protection authority.

Storage on your device

The app stores small settings on your device (for example that you've seen a message) so it works offline and doesn't repeat itself. There is no advertising or tracking by third parties.

Terms

The service

Our Household helps two people list the work their home needs and share it fairly. Setting up your home and seeing a draft plan is free of charge. Inviting your partner and using the plan together needs a subscription.

Subscription

  • Prices: per month, or per year, including VAT where it applies.
  • Free trial: the yearly plan starts with a -day free trial. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged at the end of the trial. The trial is available once per person. The monthly plan has no trial and is charged from the day you subscribe.
  • Renewal: subscriptions renew automatically for the same period until cancelled.
  • Cancelling: you can cancel any time in the app (Settings → Your subscription → Manage or cancel subscription). Your plan then runs until the end of the period you've paid for. Afterwards the household is paused: your data is kept, and everything comes back if you subscribe again.
  • Who it covers: the person who set up the household pays; the subscription covers both people in that household.

Liability

We provide the app with care but can't guarantee it is always available. Liability for slight negligence is limited to foreseeable damage typical for the contract, except for injury to life, body or health.

Right of withdrawal
